Rogenie Cordero

Founder of Computatio. 20 years in bookkeeping, tax and finance.

Rogenie Cordero founded Computatio to give small-business owners accurate, current books without hourly bills or late-night spreadsheet work.

  • 20 years of experience in bookkeeping, tax and finance
  • Dual degree, with a minor in computer science
  • More than 20 years building businesses across industries, both for-profit and nonprofit
  • Prepares tax returns and keeps her federal and California tax preparer registrations current
  • Based in San Diego, California

At Computatio, she designs the AI workflows and reviews client work. The same workflows are taught, step by step, in the free AI for Accounting guides on BookByte.
