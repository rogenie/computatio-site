About

Bookkeeping that’s fast because of AI, and right because a person checks it.

Computatio Inc. is a bookkeeping and tax service based in San Diego, California, serving clients across the United States.

Last updated November 17, 2026

What we do: monthly bookkeeping (Monthly Books, $500 a month), books cleanup ($500–$1,500 one time) and fixed-price tax returns ($350, $600 or $900).

Who we serve: owner-led agencies and service businesses with 2 to 15 people, and self-employed owners, freelancers and single-owner LLCs and S-corps.

How: AI agents sort, match and draft. A person reviews and signs off on every account and every return.

Where: San Diego, California. We work remotely with clients anywhere in the US.

Why Computatio exists

Most small businesses don’t need a finance department. They need books that are current, reconciled and explained, so they know whether they’re making money and what they’ll owe in taxes. Traditional bookkeeping is slow and priced by the hour. Software alone leaves the owner doing the work at night.

Computatio uses AI for the repetitive parts (categorizing, matching, drafting reports) and puts a person on the parts that need judgment: reviewing, reconciling, asking the right questions and signing off. That keeps prices fixed and the work accurate.

What makes us different

  • Fixed prices, published on the site. No hourly billing.
  • Closed books by the 15th, with a one-page summary in plain English and a monthly call.
  • Open about AI. We tell you where AI is used and where a person checks the work. See how we work.
  • Free guides. If you’d rather do it yourself, our AI for Accounting guides show you how, step by step.
