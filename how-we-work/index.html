How we work

AI does the sorting. A person signs off.

Here’s exactly where AI is used in your books, where a person checks it, and how your information is protected.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Each month, step by step

StepWho does itWhat happens
1. Transactions come inYour bank feedsRead-only bank and card feeds bring in last month’s transactions, or you upload statements.
2. First passAIEach transaction is categorized and matched to receipts and invoices. Anything unclear, such as a charge that could be personal, is flagged.
3. ReviewA personWe check every flagged item and ask you about the ones only you can answer.
4. ReconcileA person, with AI helpEvery bank and card account is reconciled to its statement, so totals are right, not just labels.
5. Close and reportA person signs offProfit and loss, balance sheet and cash summary, plus a one-page plain-English summary, by the 15th.
6. Monthly callYou and us30 minutes to go over your numbers and answer questions.

What AI never does on its own

  • Sign off on your books or a tax return
  • Guess about an item it can’t confirm: those go to a person, then to you
  • Send anything to you or the IRS without review

How your information is protected

  • We never ask for your banking passwords. Bank access is read-only.
  • Access to your records is limited to the people doing your work, on accounts protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • We don’t sell your information, and tax return information is used only to prepare your return. See our privacy policy.

Questions

Is my bookkeeping done entirely by AI?
No. AI does the first pass on categorizing and matching. A person reviews flagged items, reconciles every account and signs off before your report goes out.
Which software do you work in?
QuickBooks Online or Xero. If you don’t have either yet, we’ll recommend one on your first call.
When do I get my monthly report?
By the 15th of each month, for the month before.
