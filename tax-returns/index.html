What to have ready

  • Last year’s tax return
  • W-2s, 1099s (1099-NEC, 1099-K, 1099-INT and others) and any other tax forms you received
  • Business income and expenses for the year: a profit and loss report, or your bank and card statements
  • Estimated tax payments you made, with dates and amounts
  • Records for deductions you plan to claim, such as mileage, a home office, health insurance or retirement contributions

Don’t worry if something is missing. We send a checklist after you sign up and tell you exactly what’s still needed.

How it works

  1. Choose your return and pay the fixed price, or book a call if you’re not sure.
